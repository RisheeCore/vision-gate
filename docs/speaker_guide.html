“72 seeds over 7 attack classes, 63 benign of which 23 are deliberately tricky, 464 held-out payloads after mutators. Split by family so a mutator variant never sits on both sides.”

Say “classes”, not “families”, on the first number. 72 is the family count.

7 · Red-team method (45s)

“60/40 tune and held-out. A bypass only counts if the payload actually works. M13 to M15 stay out of tuning. Then: attack, find bypass, add rule, re-measure.”
“Why is 60/40 not 50/50?” — “Tune needs enough to write rules; 40% held-out still gives 464 records and a tight interval.”
“Who wrote the seeds?” — “The team, by hand, from documented failure modes — not scraped malware.”
